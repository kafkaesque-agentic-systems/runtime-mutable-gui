# UI intake: <<project name>>

Answer under each question. Leave nothing blank: write "none" or "you decide" where that is
the answer. "You decide" becomes a proposal you accept or change when you see the mockups.

## 1. People and place

1. Who looks at this product, and on what (phone, tablet, desktop browser, a plugin window, a terminal)?
2. Where and how do they use it: sitting at a desk, standing in a room, one-handed, for seconds or for hours?
3. How skilled are they with it: first-time, occasional, daily expert?

## 2. Tasks

4. The three to five things people do most, most frequent first.
5. For the first of those: what do they see first, what do they do, what tells them it worked?
6. What must be reachable in one step from anywhere?

## 3. Structure

7. The main sections of the product, as you would name them in a menu.
8. Sizes it must work at (smallest and largest), and whether it rotates or resizes.

## 4. Look and feel

9. Three words for how it should feel.
10. Products whose interface you like, and what you like about each. Products you dislike, and why.
11. Existing brand: colours, fonts, logo, icons. Light theme, dark theme, or both?
12. Dense like a professional tool, or sparse like a consumer app?

## 5. States and failure

13. What does a person see with no data yet, while something loads, when something fails, when offline?
14. Which mistakes must be hard to make, and which actions need a confirmation or an undo?

## 6. Access

15. Accessibility requirements: contrast, text size, screen reader, keyboard only, languages, right-to-left.

## 7. Tooling

16. UI toolkit or component library you require or forbid.
17. Anything else the designer must know.
